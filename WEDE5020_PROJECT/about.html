Our Story

Green Seeds Community Garden started back in 2018. A group of neighbors saw an empty, dirty lot in the middle of our city and decided it could be something better. We spent months cleaning it up and building the first few garden beds.

Today, we have 40 plots where local families grow their own food. We also have a "learning zone" where we host workshops for kids from the local primary schools to learn about where their food comes from.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission: To help our community stay healthy and connected by giving them a place to grow organic food and learn about nature.

Our Vision: We want to see a city where every person has access to fresh vegetables and a quiet green space to hang out in.
