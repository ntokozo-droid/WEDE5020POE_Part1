What We Offer

We provide the space, tools, and knowledge. You bring the passion for growing!

Garden Plot Rentals

Rent your own 2x2 meter garden plot for a small monthly fee. We provide the soil and water; you choose what to plant!

  • Organic compost provided
  • Access to shared garden tools
  • Weekly gardening tips from our experts

Educational Workshops

We host weekly classes on sustainable living. Topics include composting, seed saving, and organic pest control.
